Privacy Policy Delete Account

JTR System accounts are managed for restaurant staff by the restaurant operator (JTR POS / back office). You can request deletion of your staff account and related personal data as described below.

What will be deleted

  • Your staff login / user profile associated with the restaurant POS (where applicable).
  • Device activation binding for your phone/tablet (device credentials revoked so the app can no longer access that restaurant server).
  • Local session data on your device after logout / uninstall / clear app data.

What may be retained

Operational records required for the restaurant’s business (for example historical orders or fiscal records) may be retained by the restaurant under their legal and accounting obligations. Those records are controlled by the restaurant’s JTR server, not by the mobile app publisher alone.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to contact.jtrinnovation@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject: JTR System Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include: your full name, restaurant / establishment name, username (if any), and the email or phone linked to your account.
  4. We will confirm and process the request, typically within 30 days.
