This Privacy Policy describes how JTR Innovation (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use the JTR System mobile application for restaurant point-of-sale (POS) operations.

1. Who we are

JTR System is a professional POS companion app used by restaurant staff to connect to an on-premises or hosted JTR cash register / server. Contact: contact.jtrinnovation@gmail.com · +212 8 08 58 51 28 / +212 6 66 44 43 30

2. Information we process

  • Account / staff credentials used to sign in to the restaurant POS (username and role as provided by the restaurant).
  • Device activation data such as device identifiers issued by the JTR server, installation/device instance identifiers needed to bind the app to an authorized terminal, and technical metadata (app version, platform).
  • Operational data related to orders, tables, payments, and restaurant configuration retrieved from or sent to the restaurant’s JTR server.
  • Device permissions such as camera / photo library access when you scan or import an activation QR code, and local network access to reach the restaurant POS on Wi‑Fi.

3. How we use information

  • To activate and authenticate the device against the restaurant’s JTR server.
  • To operate POS features (orders, tables, payments, catalog, statistics).
  • To secure the installation against unauthorized cloning or reuse of device credentials.
  • To diagnose connectivity and application issues when you contact support.

4. Where data is stored

Restaurant business data is processed on the restaurant’s JTR server (local network or hosting chosen by the restaurant). The mobile app may store limited data locally on the device (for example session tokens, device activation secrets, and offline caches) using on-device storage protected by the operating system where applicable.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with the restaurant operator that manages your POS account and with service providers strictly required to operate the product (for example hosting or support), subject to confidentiality.

6. Retention

Local app data remains on the device until you log out, clear app data, uninstall the app, or until the restaurant administrator revokes the device. Server-side retention is controlled by the restaurant’s JTR deployment and policies.

7. Your choices & deletion

You may request deletion of your staff account / associated personal data via our Delete Account page, or by emailing contact.jtrinnovation@gmail.com. Restaurant administrators can also deactivate devices and users from the JTR back office.

8. Children’s privacy

JTR System is intended for professional restaurant use and is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
